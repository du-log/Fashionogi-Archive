# About Us
### If there is one true endgame in Mabinogi, it’s **Fashionogi**.

Welcome to the Fashionogi Archive, a dedicated fashion archive built entirely for the Mabinogi community. The goal here is simple: to create a centralized, easily searchable hub where players can share their creative outfits, show off unique dye combinations, and find inspiration for their next look.

Whether you are mixing and matching gacha drops or putting together a clean, classic aesthetic from NPC shops, this is the place to document it. You can browse the latest submissions and favorite the outfits that catch your eye.

This project is built by a fan, for the fans. As the community grows, the site will keep evolving with new features to make tracking down that perfect outfit combination even easier.

Happy styling!
