# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: October 2026**

Welcome to the Fashionogi Archive. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you visit our website.

## Information We Collect

- Account Information: When you register, we collect your email address and chosen username to create and manage your account.

- User-Generated Content: We store the screenshots, descriptions, and equipment tags you submit to the platform.

- Technical Data: We use strictly necessary cookies to maintain your active login session and secure your account. We do not use third-party tracking or advertising cookies.

## How We Use Your Information
We use the information we collect solely to operate and maintain Fashionogi Archive. This includes authenticating your account, displaying your submitted outfits, enforcing daily submission limits, and protecting the platform from spam or abuse.

## Data Sharing and Disclosure
We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. Your public profile, including your username and submitted outfits, will be visible to other visitors.

## Data Security
We implement standard security measures to the best our ability, including secure cookie handling and encrypted password storage, to protect your account. However, no internet transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
