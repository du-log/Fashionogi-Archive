# Rules & Guidelines
To keep the archive high-quality and useful for everyone trying to find outfit inspiration, we have a few ground rules. By uploading to The Fashionogi Archive, you agree to play by these guidelines.

## Keep Tags and Equipment Accurate
The whole point of the archive is to help people find specific items. Please make sure you are tagging your equipment, slots, and dye parts as accurately as possible. Don't troll the search system with fake item names.

## Outfits Must Be Achievable In-Game
Your submissions should reflect what is actually possible inside Mabinogi. Light visual editing for color correction or cropping is totally fine, but please do not heavily edit, mod, or manipulate the screenshots to create outfits or dye combinations that don't exist in the actual game.

## The 24-Hour Submission Limit
To prevent spam and keep the latest feed fresh for everyone, accounts are limited to 7 submissions per 24-hour period. Take your time, pick your best screenshots, and show us your top-tier looks.

## Quality Matters
Try to take clear, well-lit screenshots. Turn the UI off, find a good background, and make sure the outfit is the main focus of the image.

## Keep It Clean and Respectful
Fashionogi is a public, community-focused site. Absolutely no NSFW (Not Safe For Work) material, offensive content, discriminatory imagery, or harassment will be tolerated. Keep your profile bios, submission titles, and descriptions friendly.

## Admin Discretion
We reserve the right to reject pending submissions, remove live outfits, or ban accounts that repeatedly ignore these guidelines, try to bypass the submission limits, or generally cause trouble for the community. Just be cool, and we won't have any problems.
